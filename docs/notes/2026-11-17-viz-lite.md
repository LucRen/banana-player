# Lite: the visualizer without pictures

Lite is the cheap music style: no `lavfi-complex` graph, no decoded picture for the visualizer, no per-song card
writes. The player measures the sound with a small tap in mpv's af chain and draws a glow and twelve bars itself on the
top panel.

## How it hears the music

- `joinAf` adds `@bplite` after `@bpin` **only while Lite is the chosen style and a song plays** (one `af`, the EQ and
  effect still after it; a style change uses the same single rebuild as today's graph change).
- The tap is `aresample=ochl=stereo,asetnsamples=512`, then a lowpass copy (150 Hz) is joined beside the sound
  for astats (its bass on channels 3 and 4) and `pan` drops it again: the sound that goes on to the EQ is the main pair.
  `aresample` (not `aformat`) is what makes the two `join` inputs stereo for any file: a mono file's one channel used
  to reach `join` unmapped and ended the song. astats (`metadata=1:reset=1`) measures 512-sample frames, about 94 a
  second if mpv reports each one, and **RMS only**: `Peak_level` sat near full scale on most music, which read as one
  flat loud wall (the "weak" Lite).
- With **Visualizer only** Lite has no graph, and the graph would have carried the audio track: the player restores
  the track (`aid`) whenever the graph is empty (it used to do so only for Album art and Art + visualizer, so choosing
  Lite with Visualizer only ended the file). `vid` is `no` there, since Lite draws on black.
- `af-metadata/bplite` is watched with `observe_property` (never read on the main thread). `src/vizlite.h` turns each
  reading's RMS into 0..1 against a floor and a ceiling that follow the song (ceiling jumps up at a peak, both fall
  back over ~4.5 s; a steady tone settles mid-span), then a little compression. The beat is the bass (channels 3/4)
  above 1.4 times its own **1 s EMA** of linear amplitude, at most one each 120 ms. Silence (under -60 dBFS) for 700 ms
  stops the redraw.
- The picture is drawn by `TopScreen::drawLiteFrame` (GL, additive): a glow that grows with the bass and each beat (180
  ms fall), and twelve bars (immediate attack, 250 ms fall). Each bar is a different age and a different bass/whole-
  sound mix, so two measured bands still make the row ripple. It is capped at the `visualizer-fps` setting, and does
  nothing while paused. With Art + visualizer mpv's album art is still the background; Visualizer alone gets `vid=no`
  and black. `drawLite` **swaps the top panel** after a frame it drew: no other path of the loop draws that panel while
  Lite is the style, so Lite's own present is what puts the frame on the glass.
- The tap runs in mpv's filter chain while the sound is processed, ahead of the speakers by mpv's sound output buffer
  (`--audio-buffer`, 200 ms by default; more with Bluetooth). The meters therefore keep a short queue, and the player
  draws the reading from that lead ago (`Player::liteLeadMs`, read once from `options/audio-buffer` plus one
  af-metadata tick), interpolating between the two readings around it. It is not shifted by mpv's `audio-delay`: that
  property is a user offset, not the device's buffer, so shifting by it would hide the real lead instead of fixing it.

## The handheld's black top panel (2026-11-18)

The handheld (BananaOS, KMSDRM/GLES, the bundled mpv v0.40 + FFmpeg 7.1.1) showed nothing of Lite, and coming back to
Lite from another style the panel looked frozen. Cause: **Lite's path never presented the window.** `drawLiteFrame` only
draws into the GL back buffer, and every other top-panel path in `TopScreen::draw` (a reader, the idle icon, a video
frame, a photo) calls `SDL_GL_SwapWindow`; Lite's did not, and nothing else swaps the top window while Lite is the style.
The panel therefore kept the last frame another path had swapped: black (the idle icon's frame) when Lite was picked
first, and the previous style's graph frame after coming back to Lite — while the bottom panel kept moving, so the app
looked alive. A screenshot did show Lite because `TopScreen::capture` reads the pixels and swaps for itself, which is
why it looked right on this Mac. The af tap and `af-metadata/bplite` were fine all along: on this Mac the property
changes 16 times a second (mpv's tick), with `lavfi.astats.*` keys for channels 1-4 and the overall pair.

The fix: `drawLiteFrame` says whether it drew; `drawLite` swaps and calls `reportSwap()` when it did; and the first frame
of a Lite episode is forced, so the panel is cleared to Lite's black at once instead of keeping another style's picture.

## Delayed, weak, and a drop every second (2026-11-18)

The owner's handheld log: `lite: in 10005 ms: 180 metadata updates, 179 readings, 371 frames drawn (level 0.76, bass
0.69)`, with level 0.76-0.82 and bass 0.69-0.80 in every window. Three separate causes:

- **Weak**: the tap measured `Peak_level` as well and the player preferred RMS only when it was finite, but the level it
  drew was mapped from -60..0 dB, so most music sat at 0.7-0.8 all the time; a peak is also near full scale on almost
  every compressed master. An automatic gain (a floor and ceiling that follow the song over ~4.5 s) and RMS-only
  readings give a quiet and a loud song the same 0..1 range; the old fixed dB window is gone.
- **A drop every second**: the beat was a 1 s **box average** (`WINDOW_MS = 1000`, readings summed and divided, the
  window's edge moving every reading). Kicks every 500 ms made the threshold see the previous kick inside the window on
  alternate beats, so a 120 BPM song lit the glow every other kick — a peak that fell away once a second. The average is
  now an EMA (time constant 1 s) with no edge; the core test drives kicks every 500 ms and asserts one beat each and two
  in every second.
- **Delayed**: mpv does not raise `af-metadata` once per tap frame. It coalesces changes into its audio-chain tick:
  about 18 a second on the handheld and 16 on this Mac, **whatever the frame size** (1024 samples are 47 a second), so
  n=512 alone does not make it 90. What the picture showed was therefore a reading up to 55 ms old, held until the next,
  plus a 200 ms fall; and the tap filters 200 ms ahead of the speakers, so the picture also led what was heard. Now each
  reading is a 512-sample slice (a kick is not averaged into a 21 ms frame), the drawing interpolates between readings at
  the panel's rate, the bars attack at once and fall over 250 ms (the glow 180 ms), and the readings are shown from
  `audio-buffer` ago so they line up with what is heard. The 10 s line's `(17.9/s)` is the real delivered rate: if it
  stays near 18 on a device, that is mpv's tick and not lost readings.

The core test (`testLiteMeter`) covers the gain mapping (steady tone mid-span, immediate rise at a peak, quiet and loud
songs both full), the EMA beat on a synthetic 500 ms kick envelope (15 beats in 8 s, two per later second), and the
time queue (interpolation, nothing before the history).

## Measured on this Mac

The same generated song and window for each style, 20 s while it played, one run at a time (nothing else heavy on the
machine): macOS 26.5, Apple silicon, desktop build. (These numbers are from the 1024-sample, Peak_level build; the
2026-11-18 change costs a few `exp()` calls per reading and one more tap frame size, not another filter.)

| style | CPU (one core) | peak RSS |
|---|---|---|
| **Lite** (Visualizer) | **11%** | **127 MB** |
| Peaks Banana (Visualizer) | 25% | 145 MB |

The machine has no `ps` in this harness (the shell refuses it), so CPU is the player's own `getrusage` delta
(user+sys) over the 20 s window divided by the window, and RSS is `ru_maxrss` (peak resident set, as `ps` would show
the high-water mark). Both styles ran the same 30 s, 48 kHz stereo WAV: a steady 220 Hz tone with a decaying 60 Hz burst
every 0.5 s, 48 kHz stereo, plus a `cover.jpg` for the Art + visualizer run (that run is drawn here only by Lite's own
shapes; the art is mpv's).
