# Lite: the visualizer without pictures

Lite is the cheap music style: no `lavfi-complex` graph, no decoded picture for the visualizer, no per-song card
writes. The player measures the sound with a small tap in mpv's af chain and draws a glow and twelve bars itself on the
top panel.

## How it hears the music

- `joinAf` adds `@bplite` after `@bpin` **only while Lite is the chosen style and a song plays** (one `af`, the EQ and
  effect still after it; a style change uses the same single rebuild as today's graph change).
- The tap is `aresample=ochl=stereo,asetnsamples=1024`, then a lowpass copy (150 Hz) is joined beside the sound
  for astats (its bass on channels 3 and 4) and `pan` drops it again: the sound that goes on to the EQ is the main pair.
  `aresample` (not `aformat`) is what makes the two `join` inputs stereo for any file: a mono file's one channel used
  to reach `join` unmapped and ended the song. astats (`metadata=1:reset=1`) measures 1024-sample frames, about 47
  readings a second.
- With **Visualizer only** Lite has no graph, and the graph would have carried the audio track: the player restores
  the track (`aid`) whenever the graph is empty (it used to do so only for Album art and Art + visualizer, so choosing
  Lite with Visualizer only ended the file). `vid` is `no` there, since Lite draws on black.
- `af-metadata/bplite` is watched with `observe_property` (never read on the main thread). `src/vizlite.h` turns the
  overall RMS into the bars and the bass (channels 3/4) into the beat: louder than 1.4 times its amplitude average over
  the last second, at most one beat each 120 ms. Silence (under -60 dBFS) for 700 ms stops the redraw.
- The picture is drawn by `TopScreen::drawLiteFrame` (GL, additive): a glow that grows with the bass and each beat, and
  twelve bars (fast attack, ~200 ms fall). It is capped at the `visualizer-fps` setting, and it does nothing while
  paused. With Art + visualizer mpv's album art is still the background; Visualizer alone gets `vid=no` and black.
  `drawLite` **swaps the top panel** after a frame it drew: no other path of the loop draws that panel while Lite is the
  style, so Lite's own present is what puts the frame on the glass.
- The tap runs in mpv's filter chain while the sound is processed, ahead of the speakers by mpv's output buffer (tens of
  ms; more with Bluetooth). The picture therefore leads the sound slightly. It is not shifted by mpv's `audio-delay`:
  that property is a user offset, not the device's buffer, so shifting by it would hide the real lead instead of fixing
  it.

## The handheld's black top panel (2026-11-18)

The handheld (BananaOS, KMSDRM/GLES, the bundled mpv v0.40 + FFmpeg 7.1.1) showed nothing of Lite, and coming back to
Lite from another style the panel looked frozen. Cause: **Lite's path never presented the window.** `drawLiteFrame` only
draws into the GL back buffer, and every other top-panel path in `TopScreen::draw` (a reader, the idle icon, a video
frame, a photo) calls `SDL_GL_SwapWindow`; Lite's did not, and nothing else swaps the top window while Lite is the style.
The panel therefore kept the last frame another path had swapped: black (the idle icon's frame) when Lite was picked
first, and the previous style's graph frame after coming back to Lite — while the bottom panel kept moving, so the app
looked alive. A screenshot did show Lite because `TopScreen::capture` reads the pixels and swaps for itself, which is
why it looked right on this Mac. The af tap and `af-metadata/bplite` were fine all along: on this Mac the property
changes 16 times a second (mpv's tick), with `lavfi.astats.*` keys for channels 1-4 and the overall pair.

The fix: `drawLiteFrame` says whether it drew; `drawLite` swaps and calls `reportSwap()` when it did; and the first frame
of a Lite episode is forced, so the panel is cleared to Lite's black at once instead of keeping another style's picture.

What the log says now (a handful of lines, no flooding):

| line | when |
|---|---|
| `lite: the top panel 640x480, 45 fps: the player's own GL draws and swaps it (on black, mpv's OSD only), no mpv video frame, no graph` | Lite starts: which path draws the panel, and whether mpv's art is under it |
| `lite: af-metadata/bplite has no value yet (format 0)` | once: the observe is there, the tap's chain isn't yet |
| `lite: first af-metadata/bplite: 10 keys: lavfi.astats.1.Peak_level …` | once: the first value mpv sent, with its keys |
| `lite: in 10000 ms: 160 metadata updates, 160 readings, 312 frames drawn (level 0.52, bass 0.38)` | every 10 s while Lite is on the panel: a black panel is told apart here (0 updates: the tap is not heard; updates but no readings: the values don't parse; frames but a black panel: the present) |
| `lite: off the top panel after 130 frames (65 metadata updates): another style, or its display shows no visualizer, style Peaks` | Lite stops |

## Measured on this Mac

The same generated song and window for each style, 20 s while it played, one run at a time (nothing else heavy on the
machine): macOS 26.5, Apple silicon, desktop build.

| style | CPU (one core) | peak RSS |
|---|---|---|
| **Lite** (Visualizer) | **11%** | **127 MB** |
| Peaks Banana (Visualizer) | 25% | 145 MB |

The machine has no `ps` in this harness (the shell refuses it), so CPU is the player's own `getrusage` delta
(user+sys) over the 20 s window divided by the window, and RSS is `ru_maxrss` (peak resident set, as `ps` would show
the high-water mark). Both styles ran the same 30 s, 48 kHz stereo WAV: a steady 220 Hz tone with a decaying 60 Hz burst
every 0.5 s, 48 kHz stereo, plus a `cover.jpg` for the Art + visualizer run (that run is drawn here only by Lite's own
shapes; the art is mpv's).
