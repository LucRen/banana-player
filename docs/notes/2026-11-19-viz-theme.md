# Pocket console and Night city in the theme; Campfire across the band (2026-11-19)

Branch `viz-theme` (from main after 0.7.30). Peaks already took the theme (2026-11-18-song-display.md).

## Rules
- **Yellow is the look before themes**: in Yellow both styles emit today's constants and stills, text for text
  (`testVizTheme` pins the four graphs by an FNV-1a hash of their text, the maps' folder written as /tmp/x).
- **Pocket console** (`pocketInks`, settings.cpp): a little LCD in the theme. Screen = BG mixed with ACCENT (25% on a
  dark theme, 18% on a light one); ink = ACCENT (dark theme: a lit screen) or ACCENT 35% towards TEXT (light), then
  moved towards TEXT in 10% steps (`inkOn`) till it is 3:1 on the screen; mid = 55% from the screen to the ink; the
  grid 30% towards BG (dark) or 8% towards the ink (light). Over the art (the band on BG): the ink made 3:1 on BG, and
  ACCENT when it's 3:1 on BG too (else the ink 45% towards TEXT). Three lumas kept apart (the chroma luts tell the
  classes apart by luma). The song's lines over it alone: ink, mid (`Player::updateOverlay`).
- **Night city** (`cityPal`, viz_city.cpp): sky BG at the top to SURFACE + 22% ACCENT at the horizon, back buildings
  BUTTON, front PRESSED, unlit windows PRESSED 45% towards BG, lit ACCENT, the hottest ACCENT 40% towards DANGER, the
  moon and the stars TEXT, a roof's light DANGER. The scene is stills: each theme's are drawn once into files named
  with its id (`city4-<id>-yt.pgm` …; Yellow's keep their old names), from `cityGraph` the first time the theme
  wants them (a few ms; the maps may have been written first under another theme, so Yellow's are checked the same
  way). Over Night city alone in a theme but Yellow the song's lines are the theme's (TEXT, DIM_ON, DIM, no shadow),
  as over Peaks: its sky is the theme's BG.
- **Campfire, Art + visualizer**: the fire spans the band's 80 columns (5 a band, from column 0; the shape map's
  taper over the whole width). The same 80x25 grid and the same filters a frame; its strip's shape map is a new file
  (`fire2-as.pgm`).
- A theme change rebuilds the graph (Player::themeChanged, 500 ms later, one sound reopen); the colours are logged at
  each build.

## Log lines
- `visualizer: Pocket console in <theme>: ink #…, mid #…, screen #…, grid #…; over the art #…, #…`
- `visualizer: Night city in <theme>: sky #… to #…, walls #… / #…, windows #… (lit #…, #…), moon #…; stills ready|NOT written in N ms`
  (also printed when the lut warm-up builds every style's graph once).

## Checked
Build, core-test, one shot: Night city alone in Sakura (sky, pink buildings, plum lit windows and moon). The song's
lines there were white with a shadow in that shot; they are the theme's since (not shot again).

## What may differ on the handheld
Writing a theme's city stills on the main thread at the graph's build: a few ms on the Mac, more on the A55's card
(logged). The stills accumulate one set a theme in the state folder (~0.3 MB each, seventeen at most).
